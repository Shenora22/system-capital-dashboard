# Pilot Offer Structure

## Purpose

The pilot gives a prospect a low-risk way to see how a Lead Operations System works before investing in a full rollout.

## Scope

- One lead source.
- One central tracking view.
- Basic pipeline stages.
- Follow-up reminders or next-action fields.
- Three to five outreach or reply templates.
- Short handoff documentation.
- One review call.

## Timeline

Target delivery is 3 to 5 business days after intake materials are received.

## Success Criteria

The pilot is successful if the client can clearly see:

- Where new leads are captured.
- Which leads need attention.
- What the next action is.
- Which opportunities are active, won, lost, or in nurture.
- How the process would expand to additional lead sources.

## Conversion Path

After the pilot, recommend a full build if the client wants to connect more lead sources, add reporting, improve handoff documentation, or expand team usage.
