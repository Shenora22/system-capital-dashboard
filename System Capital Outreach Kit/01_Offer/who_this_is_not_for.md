# Who This Is Not For

This offer is not a fit for companies that:

- Do not have any leads and are not ready to start outreach or marketing.
- Want a complex enterprise CRM transformation instead of a lightweight operating system.
- Are unwilling to define ownership for follow-up.
- Do not want to share enough context about their current sales process.
- Expect software alone to replace consistent sales activity.
- Need guaranteed revenue outcomes rather than a better process for managing opportunities.

The best clients understand that the system improves visibility, consistency, and speed, but the business still needs to execute the sales process.
