# Cold Email Template

## Subject Options

- Quick lead follow-up question
- Noticed a possible lead ops gap
- Simple system for missed leads

## Email

Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed {{company_name}} is active in {{market_or_channel}}, and I wanted to ask a quick question.

Do you currently have a simple system that tracks every new lead, shows who needs follow-up, and prevents prospects from slipping through the cracks?

I build lightweight Lead Operations Systems for service businesses so owners can see their pipeline clearly, follow up faster, and stop relying on memory or scattered spreadsheets.

If useful, I can record a short Loom showing where a simple lead workflow could help {{company_name}}.

Worth sending over?

Best,
{{sender_name}}
