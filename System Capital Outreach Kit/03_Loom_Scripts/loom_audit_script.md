# Loom Audit Script

## Goal

Record a concise 3 to 6 minute Loom that shows the prospect where their lead process may be leaking opportunities and how a Lead Operations System would help.

## Script

Hi {{first_name}}, I put together a quick walkthrough for {{company_name}}.

I am looking specifically at how leads might come in, how they likely get tracked, and where follow-up could become inconsistent.

First, I noticed {{observation_1}}. If leads are coming from here, the risk is that they may sit in an inbox or notification feed without a clear next step.

Second, {{observation_2}}. This is usually where teams benefit from a simple pipeline that separates new, contacted, interested, booked, won, lost, and nurture leads.

Third, {{observation_3}}. A lightweight follow-up system could make sure each prospect has an owner, date, and next action.

The system I would suggest is not complicated. It would include a central tracker, a clear intake process, follow-up prompts, message templates, and a simple reporting view.

If this looks useful, the easiest first step would be a small pilot focused on one lead source so you can see the workflow in action before expanding it.

Thanks for watching, and I am happy to answer questions.
