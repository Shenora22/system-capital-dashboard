# FAQ Answers

## What is a Lead Operations System?

A Lead Operations System is a practical workflow for capturing, tracking, following up with, and reporting on sales leads. It connects the lead source to a clear next action so prospects are less likely to be missed.

## Is this a CRM?

It can work inside a CRM, spreadsheet, Notion, Airtable, or another tool. The offer is focused on the operating workflow, not forcing a specific software platform.

## How long does setup take?

Most pilot builds can be completed in a few business days after intake is complete. Standard builds depend on complexity, number of lead sources, and required documentation.

## Do we need new software?

Not always. The first recommendation is usually to use tools the client already has unless a gap clearly requires something new.

## What do you need from us?

The client should provide current lead sources, examples of existing tracking, sales stages, follow-up expectations, tool access where needed, and a point of contact for review.

## What happens after the build?

The client receives the system, documentation, a review call, and optional monthly support if they want continued cleanup or improvement.
