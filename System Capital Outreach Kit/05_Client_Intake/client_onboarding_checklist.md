# Client Onboarding Checklist

## Before Build

- Confirm client name, company, and main contact.
- Confirm target outcome for the system.
- Identify current lead sources.
- Document current sales stages.
- Collect existing spreadsheets, CRM exports, or tracking examples.
- Confirm tools the client already uses.
- Confirm who owns follow-up internally.
- Confirm required access or shared workspace permissions.

## Build Inputs

- Lead source list.
- Pipeline stage definitions.
- Follow-up timing expectations.
- Example prospect messages.
- Existing sales scripts or reply templates.
- Reporting needs.
- Any compliance or privacy constraints.

## Handoff

- Walk through the lead intake process.
- Review pipeline stages.
- Review follow-up fields and reminders.
- Explain reporting view.
- Confirm who maintains the system.
- Share documentation.
- Schedule optional support or check-in.
