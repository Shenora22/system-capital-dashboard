# Company Target List

Use this folder to organize outreach targets for the Lead Operations System offer.

Suggested fields:

- Company name.
- Website.
- Industry.
- Location.
- Lead source observed.
- Contact name.
- Contact role.
- Email or profile URL.
- Outreach status.
- Last touch date.
- Next follow-up date.
- Notes.

Prioritize service businesses where missed follow-up, scattered inbound leads, or owner-led sales processes are likely visible.
