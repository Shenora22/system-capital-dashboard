# Marketing Subsystem

The marketing subsystem owns brand assets, campaign copy, content strategy, and lead-capture presentation components.

## Contents

- `components/EmailSignup.tsx` — waitlist/lead-capture form component.
- `content/system-capital-content-engine.md` — content engine strategy document.
- `brand/` — brand governance notes and future source files.
- `campaigns/` — future campaign plans and launch assets.
- `../public/marketing/assets/brand-kit/` — public brand SVG assets served by Next.js.
- `../public/marketing/carousels/system-capital/` — public carousel slide assets.

## Route Compatibility

Legacy static asset routes under `/brand-kit/assets/*` and `/carousels/system-capital/*` are preserved with Next.js rewrites.
