# 30-Second Vertical Video Ad Script

## Product
**AI Lead Response + Follow-Up System**  
AI lead response automation for small businesses, coaches, consultants, agencies, and service providers.

## Offer Positioning
- **Starter:** Instant Lead Alert System
- **Pro:** AI Lead Response + Follow-Up System

## Script Structure

| Time | Scene | Visual | On-Screen Caption | Voiceover |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | Hook | Business owner sees a new lead notification, then glances at a crowded inbox. | **New lead came in. Now what?** | “A new lead is interested. But if your reply takes hours, the opportunity can cool off fast.” |
| 0:03-0:07 | Problem | Split screen: form submission, email inbox, phone alert, messy follow-up notes. | **Leads should not sit in limbo.** | “Most small teams are busy serving clients, checking messages, and trying to remember who needs a follow-up.” |
| 0:07-0:12 | System Reveal | Clean automation flow: Lead captured → Business alerted → Gmail draft created. | **Meet AI lead response automation.** | “This system captures new leads, alerts your business, and prepares an AI-written Gmail reply draft for review.” |
| 0:12-0:18 | Pro Follow-Up | Timeline visual: Day 0 reply draft, later follow-up draft. | **Initial reply + follow-up draft.** | “With the Pro version, it can also create a follow-up draft after a delay, so promising leads do not disappear.” |
| 0:18-0:23 | Human Control | Cursor edits an email draft before sending. | **You stay in control.** | “Nothing needs to sound robotic. You review, edit, and send when it feels right.” |
| 0:23-0:27 | Offer | Two clean cards: Starter and Pro. | **Starter: alerts. Pro: AI drafts + follow-up.** | “Choose Starter for instant lead alerts, or Pro for AI lead response and follow-up drafts.” |
| 0:27-0:30 | CTA | Simple product dashboard/mockup with calendar booking button. | **Respond faster. Follow up smarter.** | “If you sell services, let your lead response process work in the background. Book a quick setup call.” |

## CTA Options
- **Book a setup call**
- **See how the system works**
- **Get faster lead response without adding another app**

## Production Notes
- Keep pacing calm and confident, not frantic.
- Use clean interface shots, Gmail draft visuals, phone notifications, and simple automation arrows.
- Avoid promising guaranteed sales or revenue. Position the product around response speed, consistency, and reduced manual follow-up.
