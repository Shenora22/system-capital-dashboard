# CapCut Shot List

## Editing Direction
Clean, modern, service-business focused. Use vertical 9:16 format, subtle motion, neutral backgrounds, and clear captions. Avoid flashy hype cuts. The product should feel practical and reliable.

## 30-Second Version

| Time | Shot | Suggested Visuals | Caption | Transition |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | Lead arrives | Phone lock screen or desktop notification: “New website lead.” | **New lead came in. Now what?** | Quick push-in |
| 0:03-0:06 | Current problem | Business owner switching between inbox, CRM, spreadsheet, and notes. | **Too many places to check** | Match cut |
| 0:06-0:09 | Missed momentum | Clock overlay, unread inbox, delayed response indicator. | **Slow replies lose momentum** | Soft blur to next shot |
| 0:09-0:12 | Automation reveal | Minimal flow graphic: Lead form → Alert → Gmail draft. | **AI lead response automation** | Slide left |
| 0:12-0:16 | Instant alert | Phone or Slack/email alert with lead name, service requested, contact details. | **Starter: Instant Lead Alert System** | Pop-up animation |
| 0:16-0:20 | AI draft | Gmail compose window with a polished draft response. | **Pro: AI Gmail reply draft** | Cursor wipe |
| 0:20-0:23 | Follow-up delay | Timeline: Today → Follow-up draft later. | **Follow-up prepared after a delay** | Timeline sweep |
| 0:23-0:26 | Human review | User edits a line in the email before sending. | **Review, edit, send** | Gentle zoom |
| 0:26-0:30 | CTA | Two offer cards with simple booking button. | **Respond faster. Follow up smarter.** | Clean fade out |

## Visual Asset Checklist
- Vertical phone notification mockup for new lead alerts.
- Gmail compose mockup showing a draft, not an auto-sent message.
- Simple automation flow diagram with four steps.
- Starter and Pro offer cards.
- CTA end card: “Book a setup call” or “See how it works.”

## Caption Style
- Use short captions under 8 words when possible.
- Keep text high contrast with generous spacing.
- Use terms like **AI lead response automation**, **Gmail draft**, **follow-up draft**, and **review before sending**.
