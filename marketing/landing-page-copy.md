2. Your business receives an instant alert with the important details.

## FAQ

### Does the AI automatically send emails?
No. The Pro system is designed around Gmail drafts so you can review, edit, and approve messages before they are sent.

### Who is this best for?
Small businesses, coaches, consultants, agencies, and service providers that receive inbound leads and want a more consistent response process.

### Is this just a workflow template?
No. The product is positioned as AI lead response automation. The setup may use automation tools behind the scenes, but the value is the lead response system: alerting, drafting, and follow-up support.

### What is the difference between Starter and Pro?
Starter is the **Instant Lead Alert System**. It focuses on notifying your business as soon as a new lead comes in. Pro is the **AI Lead Response + Follow-Up System**. It adds Gmail reply drafts and delayed follow-up drafts.

### Can the drafts match my business tone?
Yes. The system can be configured around your services, preferred tone, common lead questions, and the next step you want prospects to take.

### Do I need a big CRM?
No. This is designed for businesses that want practical lead response automation without adding a complicated sales platform.

### Will this guarantee more sales?
No system can guarantee sales. This helps improve response speed, consistency, and follow-up reliability so your team can handle inbound interest more professionally.
